# ADAM Discovery Promotion Policy

A candidate may enter discovery-candidates but cannot be labeled DISCOVERY until:
- the proposition is precise and falsifiable;
- known-theorem dependencies are separated from claimed novelty;
- counterexample and adversarial tests are recorded;
- computational results are reproducible;
- proof status is explicit;
- prior-art search is documented;
- an independent reproduction exists for strong claims.

Negative results remain in the ledger.
